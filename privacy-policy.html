Remote Assist - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Developer: Nicole Wells

Contact: contact@nicolewells.co.uk

The short version

Remote Assist lets one Android device see, hear and (with permission) control another, so people can help each other. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We run no servers that receive your screen, voice, camera, messages or contacts. What you share goes directly from your device to the person you are connected with, and it is encrypted on the way.

What the app does with your information

Screen contents (only when you start screen sharing)Sent directly to the person you are helping or being helped by, encrypted. Android shows its own prompt and indicator. Not stored by us.
Microphone, camera, the phone's own sound (only when you switch them on)Sent directly to the other person during the session, encrypted. Not recorded or stored by the app.

Nothing above is sent to us, because we have no server to send it to.

Other services the app talks to

To connect two devices, the app contacts a few services that are not run by us. They see technical network information (such as your device's public IP address) in the same way any internet connection does.

  • STUN servers (Google stun.l.google.com, Cloudflare stun.cloudflare.com): used to work out how your device can be reached. They are told your public address; no session content passes through them.
  • A message relay for saved contacts (ntfy.sh by default; you can enter your own address in Settings): carries small, end-to-end encrypted connection requests so a saved contact can reach you. The relay sees your IP address and encrypted messages it cannot read. It never carries screen, voice, video or control data.
  • A TURN relay, only if you enter one in Settings (Advanced): used for networks that block direct connections. It would carry encrypted media, and is run by whoever you choose.

Please see the privacy policies of those services for how they handle network information.

Permissions

Every permission is optional and used only for the feature described.

  • Microphone: so you can talk, and so your phone's own sound can be shared. Used only when the microphone or sound button is on.
  • Camera: so you can show your camera in the video panel, and scan a QR code to pair in person.
  • Notifications: to show that a session is running (with an End button) and to tell you when a saved contact asks to connect.
  • Display over other apps: to keep the small toolbar, video panel and chat on screen while you use other apps during a session.
  • Run at start-up: to resume listening for requests from saved contacts if you had turned on "Reachable for help".
  • Foreground services (screen capture, microphone, camera): to keep a session running when you switch apps. They run only after you start a session, with a notification.
  • Accessibility service ("Remote Assist control"): see below.

The accessibility service

If, and only if, you switch on "Remote Assist control" in Android's Accessibility settings and allow control during a session, the person helping you can tap, swipe, press Back/Home/Recents and type on your phone. The service is used for nothing else. It reads the screen only to find the text box that is selected when the helper types, and it never reads or sends the contents of password fields. It does not collect or store anything, and you can switch it off at any time in Android settings or by ending the session.

Children

Remote Assist is not directed at children, and is intended for adults and for older teenagers using it with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your information, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device is removed by deleting a saved contact in Settings, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

Security

Screen, audio, video, chat and control data are encrypted between the two devices (DTLS-SRTP). Saved contact requests are encrypted and authenticated (AES-256-GCM) with a key derived from a secret that only the two devices hold. Saved secrets are stored encrypted with an Android Keystore key. The app does not allow Android backups of its data.
